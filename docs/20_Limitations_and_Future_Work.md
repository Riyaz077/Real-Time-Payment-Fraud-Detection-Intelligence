# Limitations and Future Work
## Limitations
Synthetic fraud labels may encode the generator's assumptions; the dataset is not representative of real UPI traffic; no live payment rail is integrated; anomaly scores are not calibrated probabilities; and a local simulator does not establish production streaming performance.

## Future work
Time-aware streaming, graph neural networks, calibrated gradient boosting, concept-drift detection, privacy-preserving learning, analyst feedback loops, case management, robust adversarial testing and deployment observability.
