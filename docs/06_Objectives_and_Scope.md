# Objectives and Scope
## Objectives
- Generate reproducible synthetic payment events.
- Validate data and engineer behavioural features.
- Train and compare supervised and unsupervised baselines.
- Convert model signals into transparent risk bands.
- Generate reason-coded alerts for human review.
- Expose scoring through an API and dashboard.
- Evaluate performance, latency, stability and limitations.

## In scope
Synthetic UPI-like events, local batch/stream simulation, baseline models, explainability, monitoring UI and API.

## Out of scope
Live UPI connectivity, access to bank/NPCI systems, real customer profiling, actual fund blocking, legal compliance certification, identity verification and production fraud operations.
